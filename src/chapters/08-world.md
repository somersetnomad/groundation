<div class="eyebrow">Shashamane</div>
<h2 class="h-mid">The promised land, <span class="acc-y">made real</span></h2>

In 1948 Haile Selassie granted 500 acres at Shashamane for people of the African diaspora who had supported Ethiopia against Italy. Jamaican settlers arrived from the 1960s. After the Emperor's fall, the Derg seized much of the land, and for decades many settlers lived without Ethiopian citizenship. In 2017 Ethiopia announced identity cards for them.

<div class="eyebrow">Kingston</div>
<h2 class="h-mid">Where it all <span class="acc-r">grew</span></h2>

After Pinnacle's destruction, Rastas settled in West Kingston yards such as Back-O-Wall, which the government bulldozed in 1966 to build Tivoli Gardens. The sound systems, studios and yards of Kingston carried the message out to the world.
