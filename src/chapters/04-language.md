**The "I" replaces.** The first sound of a word becomes "I", the self in unity with the divine: *unity* becomes *Inity*, *creation* becomes *Iration*.

</div><div class="prose read">

**Sound becomes sense.** If a syllable sounds negative, it is changed. *Oppressor* starts with "up", so it becomes *downpressor*; *understand* becomes *overstand*.
