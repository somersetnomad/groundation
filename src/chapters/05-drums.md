Three drums carry the music. The deep **bass** marks the pulse with heavy strokes. The **funde** holds a steady two-stroke heartbeat underneath everything. The small, high **repeater** (sometimes called the kete) is free to improvise and "talk" over the top.

The rhythm fed directly into the foundations of reggae through musicians such as Count Ossie. The playing below is a simplified sketch for the web: real players breathe and shift the time in ways a computer cannot.
