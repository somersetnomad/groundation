<div class="paths">
<article class="path" style="--pc:var(--ink)"><div class="lvl" aria-hidden="true"><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i><i></i></div><h2>Curious</h2><p class="q">"I want to understand it."</p><ul><li>Read one of the books in our <a href="/sources/">sources</a>, such as Barry Chevannes or Ennis Edmonds</li><li>Watch the documentary <em>Bad Friday</em> on Coral Gardens</li><li>Learn the meaning of the colours before wearing them</li></ul></article>
<article class="path" style="--pc:var(--gold)"><div class="lvl" aria-hidden="true"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></div><h2>Appreciative</h2><p class="q">"It speaks to me."</p><ul><li>Cook ital for a week and notice what changes</li><li>Listen past the hits: Count Ossie, the Abyssinians, Ras Michael</li><li>Buy from Rastafari makers and artists directly</li></ul></article>
<article class="path" style="--pc:var(--green)"><div class="lvl" aria-hidden="true"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></div><h2>Livity</h2><p class="q">"I want to live by it."</p><ul><li>Read the Bible as Rastas read it: Psalms, Revelation, Isaiah, Ethiopian history</li><li>Attend a public Nyabinghi Issembly or reasoning, as a guest</li><li>Adopt ital, natural living and daily meditation</li></ul></article>
<article class="path" style="--pc:var(--red)"><div class="lvl" aria-hidden="true"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i></div><h2>Commitment</h2><p class="q">"This is my faith."</p><ul><li>Spend time with elders of different Mansions before choosing a house, or none</li><li>Understand each house's discipline: Bobo Shanti and Nyabinghi expect far more than Twelve Tribes</li><li>Locks, if grown, are a covenant (Numbers 6), not a style</li></ul></article>
</div>
<div class="respect" id="respect">
<div class="do"><h2>Appreciation looks like</h2><ul><li>Learning the history before using the symbols</li><li>Crediting Rastafari when its ideas, words or music shape your work</li><li>Supporting Rasta-owned businesses, artists and communities</li><li>Asking before photographing a ceremony or a person</li></ul></div>
<div class="dont"><h2>Appropriation looks like</h2><ul><li>Using the colours or Selassie's image as a party costume or cannabis branding</li><li>Treating dreadlocks as a fashion with no awareness of their meaning</li><li>Speaking "Rasta talk" as a joke or accent</li><li>Selling the culture without any return to its people</li></ul></div>
</div>
