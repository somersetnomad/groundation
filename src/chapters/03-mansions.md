<h2 class="h-mid">A commune that still keeps the <span class="acc-y">Sabbath</span></h2>

At Bobo Hill in Bull Bay, the Ethiopia Africa Black International Congress lives much as Prince Emmanuel laid out: prayer at set hours, strict purity laws, robes and turbans, and brooms made by hand and sold in the city.

Visitors are often welcomed, but on the community's terms. If you go, ask before filming, dress modestly and follow the elders' instructions.
