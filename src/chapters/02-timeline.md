<div class="eyebrow">Easter 1963</div>
<h2 class="h-mid">Bad Friday at <span class="acc-r">Coral Gardens</span></h2>

In April 1963, in St James near Montego Bay, a violent confrontation left several people dead. What followed was a crackdown on Rastafari across Jamaica: police killed, tortured and detained Rastas, and many had their locks forcibly cut. Survivors call it **Bad Friday**.

In 2015 the Office of the Public Defender found gross breaches of constitutional rights. In 2017 Prime Minister Andrew Holness formally apologised, and a trust fund was set up for survivors. On Good Friday 2026, land titles in Albion, St James, were handed to the Rastafari Coral Gardens Benevolent Society.
