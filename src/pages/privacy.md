## The short version

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting and no social media plug-ins.

The only exception is a video you choose to play. YouTube videos stay switched off until you press play, and this page explains exactly what happens when you do.

## Who we are

Groundation is an independent educational website. For any privacy question, contact the editor at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## What this site collects

Nothing. Reading this site does not send any information about you to us or to anyone else.

## Fonts, scripts and pictures

Fonts, scripts, photographs and illustrations are all stored on our own server. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts, content delivery networks, image hosts, analytics providers or any other outside service while you read, so your IP address is not shared with them.

## YouTube videos

Each video appears as a card. Until you press **Play video**, nothing at all is loaded from YouTube.

When you press play, the video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, youtube-nocookie.com. From that moment, Google (which owns YouTube) receives your IP address and information about your browser, and may set cookies or similar storage to run the player and measure its use. Google's privacy policy applies to that: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Every card also has a **Watch on YouTube instead** link if you'd rather open the video on YouTube itself.

If you tick **Always play videos on this device**, we remember that choice in your browser so you don't have to confirm each video. You can undo it at any time on the [Your data](/your-data/) page.

## Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own, so there is no cookie banner. YouTube may set cookies only after you press play on a video.

## Hosting

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages may keep short technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors. The hosting provider's own privacy policy applies to those logs.

## Sharing features

Share cards are drawn inside your browser. "Copy text" uses your clipboard and "Share" opens your device's own share sheet; we never see what you share or with whom.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct and erase personal data. Because we hold none, there is nothing for us to provide or delete, but you can clear anything stored on your device at any time from [Your data](/your-data/). You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Children

This site is written for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. We will never add tracking without making it clear here first.
