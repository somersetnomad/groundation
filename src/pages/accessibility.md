We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we have done

- Text and background colours are chosen for strong contrast.
- Every interactive part can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
- Photographs and illustrations have text descriptions, and the map has a list of places as an alternative.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off.
- Sound and video never play unless you press play.
- The layout works on phones, tablets and desktops, and text can be zoomed to 200%.

## Known limitations

The drum section is an audio experience; its written description explains the rhythm for people who cannot hear it. YouTube videos depend on YouTube's own player and captions, which vary by video. The horizontal timeline can also be scrolled with the arrow keys.
