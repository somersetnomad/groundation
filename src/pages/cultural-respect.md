Rastafari is a living faith, born of resistance to slavery, colonialism and racism, and carried by people who were often persecuted for it. We have tried to write about it in a way that its own people would recognise.

## Our choices

- We use "Rastafari" rather than "Rastafarianism", because many adherents reject the "-ism" as a label imposed from outside.
- We present the Mansions and their disagreements without ranking them.
- We describe disputed questions from each side, in the terms that side would use.
- We use historical photographs and film only to document events, never as decoration or branding.
- We do not present cannabis as the centre of the faith. For many Rastas it is a sacrament; for some it plays no part.
- Where we can, we point to videos made or shared by Rastafari communities themselves.

## Who this is for

Curious outsiders, people drawn to the livity, and Rastafari themselves who want a well-sourced reference to share.
