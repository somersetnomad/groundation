This site collects nothing about you. Two optional features can remember things in your own browser, and both are off until you turn them on.

<div class="toggle-row"><input type="checkbox" id="optProgress"><label for="optProgress"><strong>Remember which chapters I've read</strong><span>Shows a green dot beside each chapter you've seen in the menu, and keeps your quiz best score.</span></label></div>

<div class="toggle-row"><input type="checkbox" id="optVideos"><label for="optVideos"><strong>Always play YouTube videos</strong><span>Skips the confirmation on video cards. Playing a video still loads YouTube, which may set its own cookies.</span></label></div>

<p id="dataSummary" class="muted">Nothing is stored on this device.</p>

<div class="data-actions"><button class="btn" type="button" id="clearData">Clear my data</button><span class="status" id="clearStatus" role="status"></span></div>

Clearing removes everything this site stored in your browser and switches both features off. YouTube's own cookies, if you played a video, are cleared through your browser's settings for youtube-nocookie.com. Read the full [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
